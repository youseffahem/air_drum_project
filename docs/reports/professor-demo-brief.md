**4. Automatic calibration — VERIFIED implementation.** Stable shoulder/hip references establish the body frame; navel height is an approximation. The user demonstrates six strokes per target, including at least two per hand. Measured trajectories jointly fit both columns and rows, subject to torso exclusion, local approach evidence, image bounds and minimum gaps. Another six strokes per pad must achieve ≥90% commit matching, no wrong/unmatched commits and ≥80% measured endpoint coverage. This checks internal consistency, not independent physical accuracy. **7. Strike detection — VERIFIED implementation and synthetic behavior.** Each hand needs measured downward approach, minimum travel/speed and a crossing of a pad's top surface. At most one hit occurs per continuous downstroke; measured rebound re-arms it. Bounded gaps preserve prior measured approach without inventing points. Rest, lateral/upward movement and rejected jumps do not create hits. [Strike geometry](C:/Users/jo/Desktop/air_drum_project/src/spacedrums/geometry/strokes.py)
