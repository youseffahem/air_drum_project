# Phase 18 post-session questionnaire (Experiment 3, optional)

**Status:** DRAFT instrument.

- Inclusion is an **Open Question** for the owner (time, ethics scope; pre-registration §1). If it
  is included, results are **descriptive only**: they decide no hypothesis, and no inferential
  statistics are computed on them.
- Pseudonymised: the answers are stored under the session's participant code only, never with a
  name.
- Blinding: the participant is not told which arm sounded in which block. The questions refer to
  blocks by their on-screen number (the same block numbers the participant saw).

## Part 1 — per block (asked after all blocks)

For each block 1, 2 and 3 (the order in which the participant played them):

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | How delayed did the drum sound feel compared with your stick hitting the zone? | ☐ clearly late ☐ slightly late ☐ on time ☐ slightly early ☐ clearly early ☐ cannot tell |
| Q2 | Did you hear drum sounds when you did not strike (for example during fake swings)? | ☐ never ☐ once or twice ☐ several times ☐ often |
| Q3 | Did some strikes produce no sound? | ☐ never ☐ once or twice ☐ several times ☐ often |
| Q4 | Overall, how natural did playing feel in this block? | 1 (not at all) … 5 (completely) |

## Part 2 — overall

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Q5 | Did you notice any difference between the blocks? If yes, describe it in your own words. | free text |
| Q6 | Did hearing the sound change how you moved the stick in any block? | ☐ no ☐ yes, describe: |

## Reporting rule (declared)

- Per block and arm: counts of answer categories, and the median for Q4.
- Free text is quoted anonymously, and only with consent item C1 / C1a as applicable.
- The analysis attributes blocks to arms after the session, from `live-session.json`.
- An EXPLORATORY comparison with the measured latencies (H4) may be described, never tested.
