# Participant Instructions — Space Drums recording session

**Phase:** 06 — Task 06.5 · **Version:** PI-v0.1-draft (2026-09-21) · **Status:** DRAFT; goes with Information Sheet IS-v0.1 and Consent Form CF-v0.1 (both drafts until the ethics question is answered). Not yet used with any person.

Read this sheet with the operator before the session. Ask anything that is unclear.

## What you will do

You will stand in front of a laptop webcam holding two ordinary drumsticks and play "air drums": there is no physical drum, only virtual drum zones drawn on the screen. The computer plays a drum sound when it detects that a stick tip enters a zone. During the session you will be asked to do a series of short blocks (about 10–30 seconds each); the instruction for each block appears on the screen, and the zone you should aim at is highlighted.

## How to hold the sticks

Hold them the way that feels natural to you. There is no required grip and no special rule. If you have never held drumsticks, hold them like a pencil or a hammer — whatever is comfortable.

## Where to stand

Stand inside the box drawn on the screen, at the floor mark the operator shows you, facing the camera. Keep both hands and both sticks inside the box as far as possible. One block asks you to step to a second floor mark; the operator will tell you when.

## What the cues mean

| On the screen | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Warm-up** | Move the sticks freely in the air. Do **not** hit any zone. |
| **Single hits on … (RIGHT / LEFT hand)** | Hit the highlighted zone with the named hand, about once per second. Relaxed, ordinary strokes. |
| **Alternating** | Alternate left and right hits on the highlighted zone(s). |
| **Repeated hits — metronome** | Hit the highlighted zone with the right hand in time with the tempo shown (slow / medium / fast). |
| **Rapid hits** | Hit the highlighted zone as fast as is comfortable with the named hand. Stop if it feels strained. |
| **Both hands together** | Hit the two highlighted zones at the same time, one hand each. |
| **Move without striking** | Move the sticks from zone to zone along the highlighted path **without** hitting any zone. |
| **Fake swings** | Swing towards the highlighted zone as if you were going to hit it, but pull the stick up before it enters the zone. **This is intentional** — we need examples of movements that look like hits but are not. |
| **Stop before impact** | Bring the stick down towards the highlighted zone and stop just above it, without entering. Also intentional. |
| **Crossing hands** | Play the highlighted zone while crossing your hands, or holding one stick in front of the other hand. |
| **Out of the box** | Play normally, then briefly move one hand out of the box and back. The operator will also briefly cover the camera. |
| **Second floor mark / different lighting** | The operator changes the distance or the room light; play single hits as before. |
| **Practice pad** (only if you agreed to it) | A practice pad is placed where one zone is. Hit the pad with the stick; a microphone records the taps. The operator claps at the start and end of this block. |

A countdown bar shows how much time is left in the block. If a block goes wrong (you lost track, a stick slipped), tell the operator — the block can simply be repeated.

## Things that are fine

- Hitting a zone during a "no strike" block by accident — just continue; it is noted, not a problem.
- Taking a break at any time — say so, the recording is paused between blocks.
- Asking the operator to repeat an instruction.

## Your rights

- Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason, and you can ask for your recordings to be deleted until the date on the consent form.
- Your recording is stored under a code (for example `P07`), never under your name.
- What is recorded: webcam video of your hands, arms, sticks and part of your upper body (your face may be visible), the motion data the software derives from it, session information (lighting, distance, stick colour, which blocks were done), and — only in the practice-pad block, only if you agreed — microphone audio.

Thank you for taking part.
