# Consent addendum — Space Drums live timing experiment (Phase 18)

**Status:** DRAFT addendum to Consent Form **CF-v0.1** and Information Sheet **IS-v0.1**, for
institutional review.

- Do not use before the ethics question in [`ethics-approval-note.md`](ethics-approval-note.md) is
  answered, and before the bracketed fields and this addendum are approved.
- **Version:** CF-LIVE-v0.1 (2026-09-27).
- **Consent is taken anew for every live session** (phase document, Inputs).

A live session differs from a dataset recording session (CF-v0.1) in what it is for and what it
records. This addendum lists only the differences; everything in CF-v0.1 and IS-v0.1 still applies.

## Part L — What is different in a live timing session

| | Statement | Initials |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | I understand that in this session the system plays drum sounds while I play, in several blocks. The blocks may behave differently: the sound may come slightly later, at the same time, or slightly **before** my stick reaches the drum zone. I will not be told which block is which until after the session, and I may ask for that explanation afterwards. | |
| L2 | I understand that the webcam session is recorded as in CF-v0.1, and that the system's internal timing (which drum sounds were played and when) is logged. | |
| L3 | I understand that a practice pad may be used, and that a microphone near the pad and the speaker then records the tap and drum sounds for timing only. My speech during these blocks may be captured, and it is not used. | |
| L4 | I understand that a phone camera may film my stick and hand at high speed for timing. This video is stored like the webcam video (IS-v0.1 §5). | |
| L5 | I understand that I may be asked a few questions about how the blocks felt (optional; I may skip any question). | |

## Part M — Options (tick one per row)

| | Option | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Microphone recording during the practice-pad blocks (L3). | ☐ | ☐ |
| M2 | High-speed phone video (L4). | ☐ | ☐ |
| M3 | Post-session questionnaire (L5). | ☐ | ☐ |
| M4 | My recordings from this session may also be used as in CF-v0.1 Part C (C1 / C2 / C3 exactly as ticked there). | ☐ | ☐ |

A "No" to M1–M3 only removes that measurement. A "No" to M4 means that the session is used only
for the Phase 18 timing analysis and is deleted when the project ends [date].
